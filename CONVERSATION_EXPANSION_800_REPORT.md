# Conversation expansion: 700 to 800

This is a historical batch. The subsequent expansion to 900 and current totals are
recorded in [CONVERSATION_EXPANSION_900_REPORT.md](CONVERSATION_EXPANSION_900_REPORT.md).

This batch adds **100 directly authored conversations**, IDs **`0701`–`0800`**.
Each has five complete Croatian/English exchanges. No external API or other model
was used. Situations are written individually rather than generated by substituting
nouns into shared dialogue templates.

## Coverage

- Before: **700 conversations; 4,900/8,574 forms covered**.
- Added: **100 conversations; 266 distinct previously uncovered forms**.
- Average: **2.66 new forms per added conversation**.
- After: **800 conversations; 5166/8,574 forms covered (60.3%)**.
- Still missing: **3408 forms**. Full coverage remains incomplete.
- Minimum new contribution: **two forms in every new conversation**, with no exceptions.
- Existing 700 records, including IDs, dialogue, translations, titles and order: **unchanged**.
- No previously covered vocabulary was lost; all 800 conversations still contribute
  at least two first appearances in ID order.

Counts use exact lowercased vocabulary forms in learner replies only, preserving
diacritics and grammatical distinctions. A repeated form is credited once, to the
first new conversation introducing it. Partner messages, translations, titles and
metadata do not count. Natural Croatian takes precedence over density; dialogue was
revised for flow, grammatical agreement and consistency before publication.

## Format and validation

IDs remain consecutive **`0001`–`0800`**. The next new ID is **`0801`**. Index rows
keep the compact **`c_id` / `hits`** schema. The approved sample references remain
`0070`, `0071` and `0072`; their content and occurrence weights are unchanged.

Structural validation checks unique IDs, duplicate complete dialogues, 5–8 exchanges,
bilingual messages and index consistency. An independent recount checked every
selection weight, the two-form minimum and preservation of all old coverage. The
new Croatian messages were also checked for accidental Cyrillic/replacement characters.

The writer reviewed the Croatian and translations. Automated validation does not
certify linguistic quality; the library remains a draft pending independent language
review. The current review UI still uses the existing sentence content.
