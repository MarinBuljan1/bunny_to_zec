# Conversation expansion: 900 to 1,000

This batch adds **100 directly authored conversations**, IDs **`0901`–`1000`**,
each with five complete Croatian/English exchanges. No external API or other model
was used. The original 900 records, including IDs, dialogue, translations, titles
and order, remain unchanged.

## Coverage

- Before: **900 conversations; 5,427/8,574 forms covered**.
- Added: **100 conversations; 246 distinct previously uncovered forms**.
- Average: **2.46 new forms per added conversation**.
- After: **1,000 conversations; 5673/8,574 forms covered (66.2%)**.
- Still missing: **2901 forms**. Full coverage remains incomplete.
- Every new conversation contributes **at least two uncovered forms**, with no exceptions.
- No previous coverage was lost. All 1,000 conversations still contribute at least
  two first appearances in ID order.

Counts use exact lowercased vocabulary forms in learner replies only, keeping
diacritics and grammatical distinctions separate. A repeated new form is credited
only once, to the first conversation introducing it. Partner text, translations,
titles and metadata do not contribute.

The writer reviewed the new Croatian and translations for grammar, flow and meaning.
Natural language takes priority over density. Structural checks do not certify
linguistic quality; the library remains a draft pending independent language review.

## Format and validation

IDs are consecutive **`0001`–`1000`**; the next ID is **`1001`**. Index rows retain
**`c_id` / `hits`**. Approved sample IDs `0070`, `0071`, `0072` and their weights
are unchanged. Conversation content is not yet connected to the current review UI.

The validator checks IDs, duplicate complete conversations, 5–8 exchanges, bilingual
text and index consistency. A separate recount checked every selection weight,
the two-form minimum, and preservation of all old coverage. New Croatian was also
checked for accidental Cyrillic and replacement characters.
